The Wall of Years — mockup

Every dot is a real storm, read out of this repo’s own HURDAT2 files by the app’s own parser. 3,266 storms across 252 season rows. Colour is peak Saffir-Simpson strength and it is the fixed scale — blue TD, green TS, then yellow through magenta for Cat 1–5. Tap a year to see the second screen: today’s board with the dropdown gone.
