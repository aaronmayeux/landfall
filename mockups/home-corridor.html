The corridor chart — home on top

The SVG below is generated by ui/chart-home.js, the shipping code, not redrawn for this page. Zero is the cyan line at the top and the storm rises toward it, so the closest approach is a summit rather than the bottom of a V. Each wind band grows up from the centre track and closes the gap; where one touches the home line, that line wears its colour for the hours the wind is on the house. The dashed amber is the earliest arrival allowing for track error — ours, not NHC's.

What to judge. Does the inverted axis read, or do you catch yourself re-checking which way is closer? Do three translucent bands stay legible on a phone in daylight, or turn to mud? Is the coloured segment on the home line legible enough to carry "the wind is on your house right now", given the band above it is clamped and cannot show depth? And does the dashed amber read as a hedge rather than as a second forecast?
