The other three states

Per SPEC §5 the drawer never goes blank. Unavailable, nothing bearing down and no home set are three different sentences, and a source outage must never read as an all‑clear.
Read the numbers in these four as placeholders, not measurements. Bertha is the only real payload in the repo, and she cannot be quiet, broken and Australian at the same time. The copy is the thing to judge here; the storm names and distances are stand‑ins.

What is real, what is held

Real and computed here: distance and bearing now, closest approach and its time, the interpolated wind at closest pass, peak‑vs‑arrival, the cone‑radius band, the 100‑mile window, and the watch/warning text.
Drawn dashed, not claimed: winds‑at‑home arrival and duration (needs the per‑hour wind radii walked against home — Phase B), and “your home is inside the warned zone” (needs MapServer layer 8 geometry, which is only served for a live storm). Bertha’s advisory does put Metropolitan New Orleans under a Tropical Storm Warning by name, so the product and its zone list are real — what no code can yet check is whether a specific house falls inside it.
