For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

Offshore of the Southeast U.S. Coast:
Disorganized showers and thunderstorms near the northwestern Bahamas 
and Florida are associated with a frontal system.  A non-tropical 
low pressure system is expected to form along the front near the 
northwest Bahamas on Friday.  This system could become a subtropical 
or tropical storm while it moves northward or northeastward near or 
just offshore of the southeastern U.S. coast through the weekend.  
Regardless of development, strong winds, dangerous surf, and coastal 
flooding are likely along portions of the southeast and mid-Atlantic 
coasts into early next week.  Interests along the eastern U.S. coast 
should monitor the progress of this system.  For more information 
on this system, including Gale Warnings, please see High Seas 
Forecasts issued by the National Weather Service.
