MSGID/GENADMIN/JOINT TYPHOON WRNCEN PEARL HARBOR HI//
SUBJ/TROPICAL CYCLONE FORMATION ALERT (INVEST 95W)//
RMKS/
1. FORMATION OF A SIGNIFICANT TROPICAL CYCLONE IS POSSIBLE WITHIN
120 NM EITHER SIDE OF A LINE FROM 15.6N 150.8E TO 17.9N 149.5E
WITHIN THE NEXT 12 TO 24 HOURS. AVAILABLE DATA DOES NOT JUSTIFY
ISSUANCE OF NUMBERED TROPICAL CYCLONE WARNINGS AT THIS TIME.
WINDS IN THE AREA ARE ESTIMATED TO BE 28 TO 33 KNOTS. METSAT
IMAGERY AT 110600Z INDICATES THAT A CIRCULATION CENTER IS LOCATED
NEAR 15.8N 150.7E. THE SYSTEM IS MOVING NORTHEASTWARD AT 08
KNOTS.
2. REMARKS: THE AREA OF CONVECTION (INVEST 95W) PREVIOUSLY LOCATED NEAR 
15.3N 150.0E IS NOW LOCATED NEAR 15.8N 150.7E, APPROXIMATELY 288 NM EAST 
OF SAIPAN. ANIMATED ENHANCED INFRARED (EIR) SATELLITE IMAGERY DEPICTS A 
CONSOLIDATING LOW-LEVEL CIRCULATION WITH CURVED DEEP CONVECTIVE BANDING 
AND FLARING CONVECTION TO THE SOUTH-SOUTHWEST AND NEW CONVECTION 
DEVELOPING OVER THE CENTER. TRACING OF THE LOW-LEVEL CLOUD FEATURES 
SUGGEST THAT THE CIRCULATION IS STILL OPEN IN THE NORTHWEST QUADRANT. AN 
110205Z OSCAT PASS REVEALS 25-30 KT WINDS IN THE SOUTHERN PERIPHERY OF 
THE STORM. ENVIRONMENTAL ANALYSIS INDICATES A FAVORABLE AREA FOR 
DEVELOPMENT WITH LOW TO MODERATE VERTICAL WIND SHEAR (15-20KTS), GOOD 
EQUATORWARD OUTFLOW, AND WARM SEA SURFACE TEMPERATURES (29-30C). GLOBAL 
DETERMINISTIC AND ENSEMBLE MODELS ARE IN AGREEMENT THAT FORMATION OF A 
TROPICAL DEPRESSION MAY OCCUR WITHIN 24 HOURS WHILE THE SYSTEM TRACKS 
NORTHEASTWARD BEFORE SHARPLY CURVING TO THE NORTHWEST. MAXIMUM SUSTAINED 
SURFACE WINDS ARE ESTIMATED AT 28 TO 33 KNOTS. MINIMUM SEA LEVEL 
PRESSURE IS ESTIMATED TO BE NEAR 1007 MB. THE POTENTIAL FOR THE 
DEVELOPMENT OF A SIGNIFICANT TROPICAL CYCLONE WITHIN THE NEXT 24 HOURS 
IS HIGH.
3. THIS ALERT WILL BE REISSUED, UPGRADED TO WARNING OR CANCELLED BY 
120800Z.//
