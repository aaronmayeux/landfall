For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on newly formed 
Tropical Depression Fay, located several hundred miles southwest of

Far Eastern Tropical Atlantic (AL90):
An area of low pressure located a few hundred miles southeast of the 
Cabo Verde Islands is accompanied by a large area of disorganized 
showers and thunderstorms.  This system could become a short-lived 
tropical depression on Thursday or Friday while it moves 
northwestward to northward.  However, strong upper-level winds 
should end the opportunity for development by the weekend.  
Interests in the Cabo Verde Islands should monitor the progress of 
this system as gusty winds, areas of heavy rain and flash flooding 
are possible there by Friday.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...medium...60 percent.
