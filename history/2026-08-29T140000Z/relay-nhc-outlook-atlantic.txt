Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
800 AM EDT Sat Aug 29 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

East of the Leeward Islands (Remnants of Dolly):
Showers and thunderstorms have increased this morning in 
association with the remnants of Tropical Storm Dolly, located 
several hundred miles east of the Leeward Islands. However, there 
are no signs that the system is developing a closed circulation.
Redevelopment of this system is unlikely while it moves westward to 
west-northwestward near 25 mph.  The wave could bring locally heavy 
rains and gusty winds to portions of the Leeward Islands, the Virgin 
Islands and Puerto Rico over the next several days.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...10 percent.

Northeastern and North-Central Gulf of America:
Disorganized cloudiness and showers over portions of the 
northeastern and north-central Gulf of America are associated with a 
weak area of low pressure centered about 100 miles south-southwest 
of Pensacola, Florida.  Development, if any, of this system is 
expected to be slow while it drifts westward for the next few days.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...10 percent.
