Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
400 PM PST Tue Nov 3 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Well Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (EP95): 
A broad area of low pressure located over the western portion of the 
East Pacific basin is producing disorganized showers and 
thunderstorms. Environmental conditions appear conducive for gradual 
development of this system, and a tropical depression is likely to 
form over the Central Pacific basin during the next few days while 
the system moves westward to west-northwestward at 10 to 15 mph.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...medium...50 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...70 percent.

Eastern East Pacific:
An elongated trough of low pressure is producing a large area of 
disorganized showers and thunderstorms offshore of the coast of 
Central America. Some slow development of this system is possible 
during the next few days while it drifts northward. Regardless of 
development, this system could bring areas of heavy rain to 
portions of Central America later this week and over the weekend.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...10 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...30 percent.
