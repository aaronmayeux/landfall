For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Well Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (CP95): 
Showers and thunderstorms continue to show signs of organization in 
association with an area of low pressure located over the eastern 
portion of the Central Pacific basin. While earlier satellite wind 
data suggested the system does not yet have a well-defined 
circulation, environmental conditions appear conducive for 
additional development. A tropical depression is now likely to form 
over the next day or so while the system moves westward to 
west-northwestward at 10 to 15 mph, passing well south of the 
Hawaiian Islands.

Eastern East Pacific:
A broad area of low pressure offshore of Central America is 
producing a large area of showers and thunderstorms that have not 
become any better organized over the past day. Gradual development 
of this system is possible during the next several days, and a 
tropical depression could form by this weekend while the low drifts 
slowly offshore of the Central America coast. Regardless of 
development, this system could bring areas of heavy rain to portions 
of Central America through this weekend.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...medium...40 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...60 percent.
