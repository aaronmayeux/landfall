MSGID/GENADMIN/JOINT TYPHOON WRNCEN PEARL HARBOR HI//
SUBJ/TROPICAL CYCLONE FORMATION ALERT (INVEST 96W)//
RMKS/
1. FORMATION OF A SIGNIFICANT TROPICAL CYCLONE IS POSSIBLE WITHIN
120 NM EITHER SIDE OF A LINE FROM 10.1N 161.7E TO 11.9N 158.3E
WITHIN THE NEXT 12 TO 24 HOURS. AVAILABLE DATA DOES NOT JUSTIFY
ISSUANCE OF NUMBERED TROPICAL CYCLONE WARNINGS AT THIS TIME.
WINDS IN THE AREA ARE ESTIMATED TO BE 18 TO 23 KNOTS. METSAT
IMAGERY AT 131800Z INDICATES THAT A CIRCULATION CENTER IS LOCATED
NEAR 10.1N 161.6E. THE SYSTEM IS MOVING WEST-NORTHWESTWARD AT 11
KNOTS.
2. REMARKS: THE AREA OF CONVECTION (INVEST 96W) PREVIOUSLY LOCATED  
NEAR 9.8N 162.7E IS NOW LOCATED NEAR 10.1N 161.6E, APPROXIMATELY  
240 NM NORTHEAST OF POHNPEI. ENHANCED INFRARED SATELLITE IMAGERY DEPICTS 
A LOW-LEVEL CIRCULATION CENTER (LLCC) OBSCURED BY FLARING CONVECTION. A 
PARTIAL 131046Z ASCAT (METOP-B) PASS REVEALS HEIGHTENED WIND SPEEDS OF 
20 KTS TO THE NORTHWEST OF THE CIRCULATION CENTER. ENVIRONMENTAL 
ANALYSIS REVEALS FAVORABLE CONDITIONS FOR DEVELOPMENT FEATURING LOW TO 
MODERATE EASTERLY VERTICAL WIND SHEAR (15 TO 20 KTS), WARM SEA SURFACE 
TEMPERATURES (29 TO 30C), AND GOOD EQUATORWARD AND EASTERLY UPPER-LEVEL 
OUTFLOW. GLOBAL DETERMINISTIC MODELS DEPICT 96W DEVELOPING WITHIN THE 
NEXT 36 TO 48 HOURS WHILE ENSEMBLE MODELS ARE CONSIDERABLY SOONER 
DEVELOPING INTO TROPICAL CYCLONE STRENGTH IN THE NEXT 12 TO 24 HOURS. 
BOTH MODEL GROUPS SHOW 96W TAKING A GENERALLY WEST-NORTHWESTWARD TRACK 
OVER THE NEXT 24 HOURS. MAXIMUM SUSTAINED SURFACE WINDS ARE ESTIMATED AT 
18 TO 23 KNOTS. MINIMUM SEA LEVEL PRESSURE IS ESTIMATED TO BE NEAR 1005 
MB. THE POTENTIAL FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF A SIGNIFICANT TROPICAL CYCLONE
WITHIN THE NEXT 24 HOURS IS HIGH.
3. THIS ALERT WILL BE REISSUED, UPGRADED TO WARNING OR CANCELLED BY 
141900Z.//
