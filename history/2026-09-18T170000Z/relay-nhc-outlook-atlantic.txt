Eastern Subtropical Atlantic (AL99):
Recent satellite imagery indicates that showers and thunderstorms 
are becoming better organized in association with an area of low 
pressure located several hundred miles southwest of the Azores. In 
addition, it appears that a well-defined center is developing. If 
these current trends continue, a tropical depression could form as 
soon as later this afternoon. The system is expected to move slowly 
over the next several days remaining to the southwest of the Azores.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...medium...60 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...60 percent.
