Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
1000 PM PST Sat Nov 7 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Tropical 
Storm Pena, located several hundred miles south-southeast of the Big 
Island of Hawaii.

Eastern East Pacific:
An area of low pressure located just offshore of Costa Rica and 
Nicaragua continues to produce disorganized shower and thunderstorm 
activity. Some development of this system is possible over the next 
day or so while the system drifts slowly northwestward. Thereafter, 
environmental conditions are forecast to become unfavorable for 
additional development as the system turns westward away from land. 
Regardless of development, this system is expected to bring areas of 
heavy rain to portions of Central America over the remainder of this 
weekend.
