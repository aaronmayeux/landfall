For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

Offshore of the Southeast U.S. Coast:
Disorganized showers and thunderstorms extending over portions of 
the Bahamas and Florida are associated with a frontal system.  A 
non-tropical low pressure system is expected to form along the front 
east of the Florida peninsula on Friday.  This system could become a 
subtropical or tropical storm while it moves northward or 
north-northeastward near or just offshore of the southeastern U.S. 
coast through the weekend.  Regardless of development, strong winds, 
dangerous surf, and coastal flooding are likely along portions of 
the southeast and mid-Atlantic coasts into early next week.  Heavy 
rain and flash flooding will continue for portions of the Florida 
peninsula through Friday, potentially spreading toward coastal 
portions of the Carolinas and mid-Atlantic into this weekend.  
Interests along the eastern U.S. coast should monitor the progress 
of this system.  For more information on this system, including Gale 
Warnings, please see High Seas Forecasts issued by the National 
Weather Service.
