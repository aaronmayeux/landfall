SUBJ/TROPICAL CYCLONE FORMATION ALERT (INVEST 95C) CORRECTED//
RMKS/
1. FORMATION OF A SIGNIFICANT TROPICAL CYCLONE IS POSSIBLE WITHIN
130 NM EITHER SIDE OF A LINE FROM 9.6N 140.2W TO 12.8N 149.4W
WITHIN THE NEXT 12 TO 24 HOURS. AVAILABLE DATA DOES NOT JUSTIFY
ISSUANCE OF NUMBERED TROPICAL CYCLONE WARNINGS AT THIS TIME.
WINDS IN THE AREA ARE ESTIMATED TO BE 23 TO 28 KNOTS. METSAT
IMAGERY AT 051700Z INDICATES THAT A CIRCULATION CENTER IS LOCATED
NEAR 10.5N 145.6W. THE SYSTEM IS MOVING WEST-NORTHWESTWARD AT 12
KNOTS.
2. REMARKS:THE AREA OF CONVECTION (INVEST 95C) PREVIOUSLY LOCATED NEAR 
8.4N 138.2W IS NOW LOCATED NEAR 10.5N 145.6W, APPROXIMATELY 778 NM EAST-
SOUTHEAST OF HILO, HAWAII. ANIMATED MULTISPECTRAL SATELLITE IMAGERY 
DEPICTS A BROAD LOW-LEVEL CIRCULATION CENTER (LLCC) OBSCURED BY FLARING 
CONVECTION FEATURING DEVELOPING BANDING ON THE SOUTHERN FLANK. 
ENVIRONMENTAL ANALYSIS REVEALS FAVORABLE CONDITIONS WITH LOW (10-15 
KTS) VERTICAL WIND SHEAR, WARM SEA SURFACE TEMPERATURES (28-29 C), AND 
WEAK WESTWARD OUTFLOW. GLOBAL DETERMINISTIC MODELS ARE NOT IN AGREEMENT 
IN REGARD TO DEVELOPMENT, WITH ECMWF INDICATING STEADY CONSOLIDATION 
AND GFS NOT DEPICTING THE SYSTEM REACHING WARNING CRITERIA. ENSEMBLE 
MEMBERS (ECENS AND GOOGLE DEEPMIND) DEPICT MODERATELY STEADY 
DEVELOPMENT, WHILE GEFS DEPICTS A MORE DISORGANIZED SYSTEM THROUGHOUT 
ITS LIFECYCLE. MAXIMUM SUSTAINED SURFACE WINDS ARE ESTIMATED AT 27 TO 
32 KNOTS. MINIMUM SEA LEVEL PRESSURE IS ESTIMATED TO BE NEAR 1007 MB. 
THE POTENTIAL FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF A SIGNIFICANT TROPICAL CYCLONE 
WITHIN THE NEXT 24 HOURS REMAINS HIGH.
3. THIS ALERT WILL BE REISSUED, UPGRADED TO WARNING OR CANCELLED BY
062100Z.
4. JUSTIFICATION FOR CORRECTION: CORRECTED POSITION AND INTENSITY.//
