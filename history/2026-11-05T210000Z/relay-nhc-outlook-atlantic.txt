Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
100 PM EST Thu Nov 5 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

Western and Central Atlantic:
A gale-force, non-tropical low pressure area located a few hundred 
miles south of Bermuda is producing disorganized convection to the 
north and east of its center. Environmental conditions could allow 
this system to gradually acquire some subtropical characteristics 
during the next couple of days or so while it moves slowly eastward 
over the western and central subtropical Atlantic. By late this 
weekend, upper-level winds are forecast to become unfavorable for 
further development of this system. A new non-tropical low could 
form to its east this weekend. Although environmental conditions are 
unlikely to support significant subtropical or tropical development 
of that system, gale-force winds are possible over parts of the 
central subtropical Atlantic through the weekend. For more 
information, including Gale Warnings, please see High Seas Forecasts 
issued by the National Weather Service.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...30 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...40 percent.
