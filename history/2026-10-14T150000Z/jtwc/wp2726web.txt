REMARKS:
132100Z POSITION NEAR 38.9N 158.1E.
13OCT26. TROPICAL STORM 27W (KOGUMA), LOCATED APPROXIMATELY 752
NM EAST OF MISAWA, JAPAN, HAS TRACKED NORTHEASTWARD AT 20 KNOTS
OVER THE PAST SIX HOURS. ANIMATED ENHANCED INFRARED IMAGERY (EIR)
SHOWS 27W WITH AN INCREASINGLY ASYMMETRICAL STRUCTURE AND LOSS OF
CENTRAL CONVECTION. FURTHER ANALYSIS REVEALS 27W HAS LOST TROPICAL
CHARACTERISTICS WITH AN ASYMMETRICAL WIND FIELD AND CONVECTIVE
STRUCTURE AS WELL AS EXPANDING RADIUS OF MAXIMUM WINDS (RMW).
ADDITIONALLY, THE SYSTEM IS CURRENTLY WITHIN LESS THAN 24 C SEA
SURFACE TEMPERATURES AND 30-40 KTS OF VERTICAL WIND SHEAR WHICH
CONTINUES TO ENTRAIN DRY AIR INTO THE CORE. THE SYSTEM IS ASSESSED
TO HAVE BEGUN SUBTROPICAL TRANSITION (STT) AND IS EXPECTED TO
COMPLETE WITHIN 12 HOURS, WITH LIKELY SUBSEQUENT EXTRATROPICAL
TRANSITION (ETT) AS THE SYSTEM NEARS THE UPPER LEVEL JET AND
STRONGER BAROCLINIC ZONE. THE SYSTEM IS EXPECTED TO STEADILY WEAKEN
AS IT TRANSITIONS DUE TO THE HOSTILE ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS. THIS
IS THE FINAL WARNING ON THIS SYSTEM BY THE JOINT TYPHOON WRNCEN
PEARL HARBOR HI. THE SYSTEM WILL BE CLOSELY MONITORED FOR SIGNS OF
REGENERATION. MINIMUM CENTRAL PRESSURE AT 131800Z IS 988MB. MAXIMUM
SIGNIFICANT WAVE HEIGHT AT 131800Z IS 24 FEET. REFER TO TROPICAL
DEPRESSION 28W (TWENTYEIGHT) WARNINGS (WTPN31 PGTW) FOR SIX-HOURLY
UPDATES.
