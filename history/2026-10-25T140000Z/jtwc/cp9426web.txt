SUBJ/TROPICAL CYCLONE FORMATION ALERT (INVEST 94C)//
RMKS/
1. FORMATION OF A SIGNIFICANT TROPICAL CYCLONE IS POSSIBLE WITHIN
140 NM EITHER SIDE OF A LINE FROM 14.3N 179.9W TO 14.5N 175.5W
WITHIN THE NEXT 12 TO 24 HOURS. AVAILABLE DATA DOES NOT JUSTIFY
ISSUANCE OF NUMBERED TROPICAL CYCLONE WARNINGS AT THIS TIME.
WINDS IN THE AREA ARE ESTIMATED TO BE 28 TO 33 KNOTS. METSAT
IMAGERY AT 251200Z INDICATES THAT A CIRCULATION CENTER IS LOCATED
NEAR 14.3N 179.8W. THE SYSTEM IS MOVING EASTWARD AT 06 KNOTS.
2. REMARKS: AN AREA OF CONVECTION (INVEST 94C) IS LOCATED NEAR 14.3N
179.8W, APPROXIMATELY 611NM WEST-SOUTHWEST OF JOHNSTON ATOLL, UNITED 
STATES. ANIMATED ENHANCED INFRARED IMAGERY (EIR) DEPICTS A QUICKLY 
CONSOLIDATING LOW LEVEL CIRCULATION CENTER (LLCC) WITH CURVED DEEP 
CONVECTIVE BANDING. A 250816Z ASCAT-B PASS REVEALS A DEFINED LLCC WITH 
WINDS UP TO 30KTS ON THE WESTERN SIDE OF THE SYSTEM. ENVIRONMENTAL 
ANALYSIS INDICATES FAVORABLE CONDITIONS FOR DEVELOPMENT WITH MODERATE 
VERTICAL WIND SHEAR (15-20KTS), STRONG POLEWARD OUTFLOW, AND WARM SEA 
SURFACE TEMPERATURES (28-29C). THE SYSTEM IS ON THE CUSP OF BECOMING A 
TROPICAL CYCLONE (TC), AND GLOBAL DETERMINISTIC AND ENSEMBLE MODELS 
AGREE THAT TC FORMATION WILL OCCUR WITHIN 24 HOURS. MAXIMUM SUSTAINED 
SURFACE WINDS ARE ESTIMATED AT 28 TO 33 KNOTS. MINIMUM SEA LEVEL 
PRESSURE IS ESTIMATED TO BE NEAR 1006 MB. THE POTENTIAL FOR THE 
DEVELOPMENT OF A SIGNIFICANT TROPICAL CYCLONE WITHIN THE NEXT 24 HOURS 
IS HIGH.
3. THIS ALERT WILL BE REISSUED, UPGRADED TO WARNING OR CANCELLED BY 
261430Z.//
