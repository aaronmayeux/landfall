Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
700 PM EST Fri Nov 6 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

Western and Central Atlantic (AL94):
A non-tropical low pressure area located several hundred miles south 
of Bermuda is producing disorganized showers and thunderstorms. The 
low could still acquire some subtropical characteristics through 
Saturday before upper-level winds increase on Sunday. The low is 
forecast to move generally eastward over the western and central 
subtropical Atlantic during the next couple of days and become 
absorbed by a new non-tropical low pressure system to its east early 
next week. Regardless, gale-force winds are likely over parts of the 
central subtropical Atlantic through the weekend. For more 
information, including Gale Warnings, see High Seas Forecasts issued 
by the National Weather Service.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...10 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...10 percent.
