For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

South-Southwest of Southwestern Mexico (EP99):
A broad area of low pressure located well south-southwest of the

Western East Pacific (EP98):
Showers and thunderstorms associated with an area of low pressure 
located well southwest of the southern tip of the Baja California 
Peninsula remain disorganized. Environmental conditions are 
conducive for development, and a tropical depression is likely to 
form this weekend as it moves westward at 10 to 15 mph across the 
western East Pacific.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...70 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...80 percent.

Well East-Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (Former TD 15-E):
Showers and thunderstorms associated with the remnants of Tropical 
Depression Fifteen-E, located well east of the Hawaiian Islands, 
remain disorganized. Environmental conditions appear conducive for 
some development of this system next week as it moves westward to 
west-southwestward, and a tropical depression could form well to the 
south or southeast of the Hawaiian Islands by the middle to latter 
part of next week.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...10 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...40 percent.
