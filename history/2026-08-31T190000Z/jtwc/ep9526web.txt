1. FORMATION OF A SIGNIFICANT TROPICAL CYCLONE IS POSSIBLE WITHIN
130 NM EITHER SIDE OF A LINE FROM 11.6N 105.9W TO 13.9N 107.0W
WITHIN THE NEXT 12 TO 24 HOURS. AVAILABLE DATA DOES NOT JUSTIFY
ISSUANCE OF NUMBERED TROPICAL CYCLONE WARNINGS AT THIS TIME.
WINDS IN THE AREA ARE ESTIMATED TO BE 18 TO 23 KNOTS. METSAT
IMAGERY AT 301800Z INDICATES THAT A CIRCULATION CENTER IS LOCATED
NEAR 12.0N 106.0W. THE SYSTEM IS QUASI-STATIONARY.
2. REMARKS: AN AREA OF CONVECTION (INVEST 95E) IS LOCATED NEAR 12.0N 
106.0W, APPROXIMATELY 460NM SOUTHWEST OF ACAPULCO, MEXICO. MULTISPECTRAL 
SATELLITE IMAGERY (MSI) DEPICTS A LARGE SWATH OF PERSISTENT DEEP 
CONVECTION TO THE SOUTH-SOUTHWEST OF ASSESSED CENTER. RECENT ASCAT 
WINDSPEED DATA REVEALS A POORLY CONSOLIDATED LOW-LEVEL CENTER WITH 
ELEVATED 30 KT WINDS DISPLACED TO THE NORTH. ENVIRONMENTAL ANALYSIS 
INDICATES LOW VERTICAL WIND SHEAR (10-15KTS), WEAK EQUATORWARD OUTFLOW, 
AND A FAVORABLE SEA SURFACE TEMPERATURE (29-30C). GLOBAL DETERMINISTIC 
AND ENSEMBLE MODELS ARE IN AGREEMENT OF A NORTHWESTWARD TRACK AND GRADUAL 
INTENSIFICATION OVER THE NEXT 24-48 HOURS. MAXIMUM SUSTAINED SURFACE 
WINDS ARE ESTIMATED AT 18 TO 23 KNOTS. MINIMUM SEA LEVEL PRESSURE IS 
ESTIMATED TO BE NEAR 1005 MB. THE POTENTIAL FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF A 
SIGNIFICANT TROPICAL CYCLONE WITHIN THE NEXT 24 HOURS IS HIGH.
3. THIS ALERT WILL BE REISSUED, UPGRADED TO WARNING OR CANCELLED BY 
312000Z.//
