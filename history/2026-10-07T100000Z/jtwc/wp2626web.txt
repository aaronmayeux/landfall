REMARKS:
062100Z POSITION NEAR 37.9N 151.3E.
06OCT26. TYPHOON 26W (CHOI-WAN), LOCATED APPROXIMATELY 461 NM
EAST-SOUTHEAST OF MISAWA, JAPAN, HAS TRACKED NORTHEASTWARD AT 30
KNOTS OVER THE PAST SIX HOURS. ANIMATED ENHANCED INFRARED SATELLITE 
IMAGERY (EIR) DEPICTS 26W WITH A REMNANT, COMPACT INNER CORE, WHICH IS 
UNDERGOING A RAPID DECOUPLING WITH THE LOWER-LEVEL VORTEX DUE TO 
PERSISTENT, HIGH SOUTHWESTERLY SHEAR. THE DECOUPLING HAS NOT YET 
PROGRESSED FAR ENOUGH TO SEE CLEAR EVIDENCE OF IT IN THE EIR, AND A 
LACK OF RECENT MICROWAVE IMAGERY OR SCATTEROMETER DATA LIMITS ANALYSIS 
CONFIDENCE. HOWEVER, THE OUTER EDGES OF THE LOW-LEVEL BANDING FEATURES 
CAN BE SEEN STARTING TO EMERGE ON THE SOUTHWESTERN EDGE OF THE SMALL 
CDO FEATURE WHICH IS VERY RAPIDLY TRANSLATING OFF TO THE NORTHEAST. 
PHASE-SPACE ANALYSES SUGGEST 26W IS ALREADY BEGINNING THE PROCESS OF 
EXTRATROPICAL TRANSITION (ETT) AND ITS TRANSITION IS EXEPECTED TO 
OCCUR VERY RAPIDLY NOW THAT IT HAS COMMENCED. THE SYSTEM WILL CONTINUE 
TO ACCELERATE NORTHEASTWARD THROUGH THE FORECAST PERIOD, REACHING OVER 
50 KNOTS OF FORWARD SPEED BY THE END OF THE FORECAST. IN TERMS OF 
INTENSITY, WHILE THE BULK OF THE SUBJECTIVE AND OBJECTIVE INTENSITY 
ESTIMATES HAVE BEGUN A SHARP DECLINE DUE TO THE DECREASE IN DEEP 
CONVECTIVE ACTIVITY, THE SATCON REMAINS NEAR 77 KNOTS WHICH IS VERY 
LIKELY VERY CLOSE TO REALITY, AS THE SYSTEM WILL TAKE TIME TO SPIN 
DOWN. THE ADDITION OF BAROCLINIC FORCINGS WILL SLOW THE RATE OF 
WEAKENING EVEN MORE, AND 26W IS FORECAST TO REMAIN AT TYPHOON STRENGTH 
AS IT COMPLETES ETT WITHIN THE NEXT 12 HOURS.
THIS IS THE FINAL WARNING ON THIS SYSTEM BY THE JOINT TYPHOON
WRNCEN PEARL HARBOR HI. THE SYSTEM WILL BE CLOSELY MONITORED FOR
SIGNS OF REGENERATION. MINIMUM CENTRAL PRESSURE AT 061800Z IS 959
MB. MAXIMUM SIGNIFICANT WAVE HEIGHT AT 061800Z IS 42 FEET.
