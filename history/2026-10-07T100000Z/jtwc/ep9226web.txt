IMAGERY AT 061800Z INDICATES THAT A CIRCULATION CENTER IS LOCATED
NEAR 13.0N 98.7W. THE SYSTEM IS MOVING WEST-NORTHWESTWARD AT 05
KNOTS.

96.4W, APPROXIMATELY 337NM SOUTHEAST OF ACAPULCO, MEXICO.ANIMATED

CIRCULATION CENTER AND DEEP CONVECTION BEGINNING TO BUILD OVER THE 
CENTER WITH HIGHER WINDS (20-25KTS) ALONG THE NORTHERN PERIPHERY. 
ENVIRONMENTAL ANALYSIS INDICATES 92E IS IN OVERALL FAVORABLE 
CONDITIONS FOR DEVELOPMENT DEFINED BY FAIR DIFFLUENT OUTFLOW ALOFT, LOW 
(05-10KTS) VERTICAL WIND SHEAR, AND VERY WARM (29-30C) SEA SURFACE 
TEMPERATURES. GLOBAL MODELS ARE IN AGREEMENT THAT INVEST 92E WILL MOVE 
GENERALLY WEST-NORTHWESTWARD OVER THE NEXT 24-48 HOURS. MAXIMUM 
SUSTAINED SURFACE WINDS ARE ESTIMATED AT 23 TO 28 KNOTS. MINIMUM SEA 
LEVEL PRESSURE IS ESTIMATED TO BE NEAR 1001 MB. THE POTENTIAL FOR THE 
DEVELOPMENT OF A SIGNIFICANT TROPICAL CYCLONE WITHIN THE NEXT 24 HOURS 
REMAINS HIGH. THIS SUPERSEDES REF A (WTPN21 PHNC 051900).
3. THIS ALERT WILL BE REISSUED, UPGRADED TO WARNING OR CANCELLED BY
