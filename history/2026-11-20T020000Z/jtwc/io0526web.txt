192100Z POSITION NEAR 17.7N 84.6E. 19NOV26. TROPICAL CYCLONE 05B 
(AKVAN), LOCATED APPROXIMATELY 385 NM SOUTH-SOUTHWEST OF KOLKATA, 
INDIA, HAS TRACKED NORTH-NORTHWESTWARD AT 08 KNOTS OVER THE PAST SIX 
HOURS. ANIMATED SHORTWAVE INFRARED (SWIR) SATELLITE IMAGERY DEPICTS A 
HIGHLY ASYMMETRIC SYSTEM, WITH AN EXPOSED LOW LEVEL CIRCULATION ENTER 
(LLCC) POSITIONED ON THE SOUTHWESTERN EDGE OF AN AREA OF FLARING DEEP 
CONVECTION. A 191445Z ASCAT-B PASS REVEALED A HIGHLY ELONGATED AND 
WEAK LLCC, WITH 20-25 KNOT WINDS SURROUNDING THE BULK OF THE LLCC, 
AND 25-30 KNOT WINDS LOCATED ALONG THE COASTLINE TO THE NORTH AND 
UNDER THE CONVECTION TO THE NORTHEAST. REANALYSIS OF EARLIER ASCAT 
DATA SUPPORTS A DOWNGRADE OF THE SYSTEM TO A TROPICAL DEPRESSION AS 
EARLY AS 190600Z. ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS ARE GENERALLY FAVORABLE, 
BUT STRONGLY MITIGATED BY THE BROAD NATURE OF THE LLCC. WITH THE 
LIMITED TIME REMAINING OVER WATER, THE SYSTEM IS HIGHLY UNLIKELY TO 
HAVE ENOUGH TIME TO CONSOLIDATE OR INTENSIFY PRIOR TO LANDFALL. TC  
5B WILL TRACK NORTH-NORTHWESTWARD ALONG THE WESTERN SIDE OF THE 
SUBTROPICAL RIDGE (STR) FAR TO THE EAST, THROUGH THE FORECAST PERIOD. 
LANDFALL ALONG THE EASTERN COAST OF INDIA IS EXPECTED WITHIN THE NEXT 
12 HOURS, AFTER WHICH THE SYSTEM WILL CONTINUE INLAND ON A NORTHWARD 
TRAJECTORY. TC 05B WILL REMAIN BELOW WARNING CRITERIA THROUGH 
LANDFALL, AND THEN CONTINUE TO WEAKEN RAPIDLY AFTER MOVING INLAND. 
THIS IS THE FINAL WARNING ON THIS SYSTEM BY THE JOINT TYPHOON WRNCEN 
PEARL HARBOR HI. THE SYSTEM WILL BE CLOSELY MONITORED FOR SIGNS OF 
REGENERATION. MINIMUM CENTRAL PRESSURE AT 191800Z IS 1007 MB. MAXIMUM 
SIGNIFICANT WAVE HEIGHT AT 191800Z IS 15 FEET.//
