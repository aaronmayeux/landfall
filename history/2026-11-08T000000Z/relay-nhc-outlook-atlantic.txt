Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
100 PM EST Sat Nov 7 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

Western and Central Atlantic (AL94):
A non-tropical low pressure area located several hundred miles 
south-southeast of Bermuda is producing disorganized showers and 
thunderstorms. Recent satellite-derived wind data indicates a 
sharpening frontal structure is associated with this system. The low 
could still briefly acquire some subtropical characteristics through 
tonight, though this scenario appears to be trending less likely. 
The low is forecast to move generally eastward over the western and 
central subtropical Atlantic during the next couple of days and 
become absorbed by a new non-tropical low pressure system to its 
east early next week. Regardless, gale-force winds are likely over 
parts of the central subtropical Atlantic through the weekend. For 
more information, including Gale Warnings, see High Seas Forecasts 
issued by the National Weather Service.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...10 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...10 percent.
