1. FORMATION OF A SIGNIFICANT TROPICAL CYCLONE IS POSSIBLE WITHIN

NEAR 12.0N 108.6W. THE SYSTEM IS MOVING WEST-NORTHWESTWARD AT 12
KNOTS.
2. REMARKS: AN AREA OF CONVECTION (INVEST 92E) IS LOCATED NEAR 12.0N 
108.6W, APPROXIMATELY 423NM SOUTH-SOUTHEAST OF SOCORRO. ANIMATED 
ENHANCED INFRARED IMAGERY (EIR) DEPICTS A BROAD DEVELOPING LOW LEVEL 
CIRCULATION CENTER (LLCC) OBSCURED BY FLARING CONVECTION OVER THE NORTH 
EASTERN PERIPHERY OF THE CIRCULATION. ENVIRONMENTAL ANALYSIS REVEALS 
FAVORABLE CONDITIONS FOR DEVELOPMENT WITH LOW (10 TO 15 KTS) VERTICAL 
WIND SHEAR, WARM (28 TO 29 C) SEA SURFACE TEMPERATURES, AND EQUATORWARD 
UPPER-LEVEL OUTFLOW. GLOBAL DETERMINISTIC MODELS SHOW THE CONTINUED 
DEVELOPMENT OF 92E INTO A TROPICAL STORM NEAR INVEST 91E INTO A LARGER 
SPINNER. ENSEMBLE MODELS ALSO SHOW THE CONTINUED DEVELOPMENT OF 92E AND 
FOR IT TO TAK A GENERAL WEST-NORTHWESTERN TRACK OVER THE NEXT 24 TO 48 
HOUR PERIOD. MAXIMUM SUSTAINED SURFACE WINDS ARE ESTIMATED AT 17 TO 22 
KNOTS. MINIMUM SEA LEVEL PRESSURE IS ESTIMATED TO BE NEAR 1006 MB. THE
POTENTIAL FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF A SIGNIFICANT TROPICAL CYCLONE
WITHIN THE NEXT 24 HOURS IS HIGH.
3. THIS ALERT WILL BE REISSUED, UPGRADED TO WARNING OR CANCELLED BY
