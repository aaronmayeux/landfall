301500Z POSITION NEAR 34.6N 165.8E.
30AUG26. TROPICAL STORM 20W (BANG-LANG), LOCATED APPROXIMATELY
889 NM NORTH OF WAKE ISLAND, HAS TRACKED NORTHWESTWARD AT 14
KNOTS OVER THE PAST SIX HOURS. THE SYSTEM HAS CONTINUED TO RAPIDLY
WEAKEN AS IT INTERACTS WITH TROPICAL DEPRESSION 21W LOCATED 180 NM TO
THE SOUTH. DEEP CONVECTION PERSISTS ALONG THE NORTHERN AND EASTERN
PERIPHERY. THE SYSTEM WILL UNDERGO SUBTROPICAL TRANSITION AS IT
TRAVERSES SEA SURFACE TEMPERATURES BELOW 26 DEGREES CELSIUS,
WHILE AN UPPER-LEVEL TROUGH RESIDES OVERHEAD. AS THE PROXIMITY TO
TD 21W INCREASES, AN EXTENSIVE RIDGE CENTERED EAST OF THE DATELINE
WILL PULL THE SYSTEM NORTHWARD OVER COOLER WATERS AND INTO A MORE
BAROCLINIC ENVIRONMENT. THE FORECAST CALLS FOR SLOW WEAKENING AS
CONVECTION PERSISTS DUE TO A COLDER TROPOPAUSE AND STRONG POLEWARD
OUTFLOW ALOFT. THIS IS THE FINAL WARNING ON THIS SYSTEM BY THE JOINT 
TYPHOON WRNCEN PEARL HARBOR HI. THE SYSTEM WILL BE CLOSELY MONITORED 
FOR SIGNS OF REGENERATION. MINIMUM CENTRAL PRESSURE AT 301200Z IS 993
MB. MAXIMUM SIGNIFICANT WAVE HEIGHT AT 301200Z IS 12 FEET.
