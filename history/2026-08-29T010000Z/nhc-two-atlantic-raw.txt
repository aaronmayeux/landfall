For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

East of the Leeward Islands (Remnants of Dolly):
A tropical wave, the remnants of Tropical Storm Dolly, are located

rains and gusty winds to portions of the Leeward Islands, the 
Virgin Islands and Puerto Rico over the next several days.
