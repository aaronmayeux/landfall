1. FORMATION OF A SIGNIFICANT TROPICAL CYCLONE IS POSSIBLE WITHIN

NEAR 9.6S 163.4W. THE SYSTEM IS MOVING SOUTHWARD AT 06 KNOTS.
2. REMARKS: THE AREA OF CONVECTION (INVEST 92P) PREVIOUSLY LOCATED 
NEAR 9.0S 163.5W IS NOW LOCATED NEAR 9.6S 163.4W, APPROXIMATELY 512 NM 
EAST-NORTHEAST OF PAGO PAGO. ANIMATED ENHANCED INFRARED SATELLITE 
IMAGERY AND A 151128Z 89 GHZ AMSR3 MICROWAVE IMAGE DEPICTS A FULLY 
OBSCURED LOWER-LEVEL CIRCULATION CENTER WITH PERSISTENT DEEP 
CONVECTION BUILDING OVER THE CENTER AND PROMINENT FORMATIVE BANDING 
ALONG THE EASTERN PORTION OF 92P. A 151126Z OSCAT PASS EMPHASIZES 
CONSOLIDATING CLOSED CIRCULATION WITH ELEVATED (25-30KT) WINDS ALONG 
THE NORTHERN AND SOUTHERN PERIPHERY. ENVIRONMENTAL ANALYSIS REVEALS A 
FAVORABLE ENVIRONMENT FOR DEVELOPMENT WITH STRONG UPPER-LEVEL OUTFLOW, 
LOW VERTICAL WIND SHEAR (5-10 KTS), AND WARM SEA SURFACE TEMPERATURES 
(28-29C). GLOBAL DETERMINISTIC MODELS ARE IN AGREEMENT THAT 92P WILL 
CONTINUE TO CONSOLIDATE AND INTENSIFY OVER THE NEXT 24 HOURS, HOWEVER, 
ALL DETERMINISTIC AND ENSEMBLE MODELS ARE IN FAVOR OF 92P MOVING 
SOUTHEASTWARD INTO EXCEPTIONALLY COLD WATER PAST 72 HOURS. MAXIMUM 
SUSTAINED SURFACE WINDS ARE ESTIMATED AT 28 TO 33 KNOTS. MINIMUM SEA 
LEVEL PRESSURE IS ESTIMATED TO BE NEAR 998 MB. THE POTENTIAL FOR THE 
DEVELOPMENT OF A SIGNIFICANT TROPICAL CYCLONE WITHIN THE NEXT 24 HOURS 
IS HIGH.
3. THIS ALERT WILL BE REISSUED, UPGRADED TO WARNING OR CANCELLED BY 
161800Z.//
