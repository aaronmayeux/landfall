Eastern East Pacific (EP96):
Recent satellite wind data indicates that a small area of low 
pressure located more than one hundred miles offshore of Nicaragua

of it becoming a short-lived tropical cyclone are decreasing. 
Regardless, this system is expected to bring periods of heavy rain 
to portions of Central America over the next day or so.
