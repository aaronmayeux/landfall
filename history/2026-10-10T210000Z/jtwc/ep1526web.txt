KNOTS OVER THE PAST SIX HOURS. TS 15E HAS TURNED WESTWARD, AGAINST
THE PREVIOUSLY FORECASTED RECURVE TOWARD THE NORTHEAST. THE
SYSTEM HAS WEAKENED QUICKLY AND BECOME SHALLOW, NOW DRIVEN BY

OVER THE WATERS OFF THE COAST OF CENTRAL HONSHU AT THE END OF
THE FORECAST PERIOD.
