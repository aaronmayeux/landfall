Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
100 AM EST Fri Nov 6 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

Western and Central Atlantic:
A gale-force, non-tropical low pressure area located a few hundred 
miles south of Bermuda is producing an area of cloudiness and 
showers. This system could acquire some subtropical characteristics 
during the next couple of days while it moves slowly eastward over 
the western and central subtropical Atlantic. By late this weekend, 
upper-level winds are forecast to become unfavorable for further 
development. While a new non-tropical low could form from the 
remnants of the current low early next week, environmental 
conditions are unlikely to support significant development. 
Regardless, gale-force winds are likely over parts of the central 
subtropical Atlantic through the weekend. For more information, 
including Gale Warnings, please see High Seas Forecasts issued by 
the National Weather Service.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...20 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...30 percent.
