Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
1000 PM PST Thu Nov 5 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Eastern Central Pacific (CP95): 
Showers and thunderstorms associated with an area of low pressure 
located several hundred miles to the southeast of the Big Island of 
Hawaii continue to show signs of organization.  Environmental 
conditions are conducive for additional development, and a tropical 
depression is expected to develop on Friday.  The system is 
forecast to move west-northwestward to westward at 10 to 15 mph, 
passing well south of the Hawaiian Islands. 
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...90 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...90 percent.

Eastern East Pacific:
A broad area of low pressure offshore of Central America is 
producing a disorganized area of showers and thunderstorms.  A 
tropical depression could still form over the weekend while the low 
drifts slowly offshore.  Upper-level winds should prevent further 
development after the weekend.  This system could bring areas of 
heavy rain to portions of Central America through the weekend.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...medium...40 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...40 percent.
