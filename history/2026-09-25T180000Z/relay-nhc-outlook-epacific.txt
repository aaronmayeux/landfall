Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
1100 AM PDT Fri Sep 25 2026

For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane 
Polo, located a few hundred miles southwest of Cabo Corrientes, 
Mexico, on Hurricane Odalys, located well west-southwest of the 
southern tip of Baja California, and on Hurricane Nolo, located 
a couple hundred miles south of the Big Island of Hawaii.

South of the Gulf of Tehuantepec:
An area of low pressure is expected to form in a day or so to the 
south of the Gulf of Tehuantepec.  Afterward, environmental 
conditions appear favorable for gradual development, and a tropical 
depression is expected to form late this weekend or early next week 
while the system moves slowly west-northwestward to northwestward, 
parallel to the coast of southern and southwestern Mexico.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...medium...50 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...90 percent.

Well East-Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands:
A broad area of low pressure is forecast to form about 1000 miles 
east-southeast of the Hawaiian Islands over the weekend. 
Environmental conditions appear favorable for gradual development of 
this system, and a tropical depression could form early next week.  
The system is forecast to move generally northeastward, remaining 
well east-southeast of the Hawaiian Islands.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...20 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...medium...60 percent.
