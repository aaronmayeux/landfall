Far Eastern Tropical Atlantic (AL90):
A broad area of low pressure located a few hundred miles 
south-southeast of the Cabo Verde Islands is producing a large 
area of disorganized showers and thunderstorms.  This system could 
become a short-lived tropical depression or a tropical storm later 
today or on Friday while it moves northwestward to northward.  
Increasing upper-level winds, however, should prevent further 
development by the weekend.  Interests in the Cabo Verde Islands 
should monitor the progress of this system as gusty winds, areas of 
heavy rain, and flash flooding are possible over the islands by 
Friday.
