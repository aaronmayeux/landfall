Tropical Weather Outlook
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
700 AM EST Sat Nov 21 2026

For the North Atlantic...Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of America:

Offshore of the Southeast U.S. Coast:
A large non-tropical low pressure area centered a few hundred miles 
east of northeastern Florida continues to produce disorganized 
showers and thunderstorms. The system is moving into an 
environment that will probably not be conducive for development of 
significant tropical or subtropical characteristics. 

Although the chances of tropical development continue to decrease, 
this large weather system is still expected to produce gusty winds, 
dangerous surf, rip currents, and coastal flooding along portions 
of the southeast and mid-Atlantic U.S. coasts into early next week. 
In addition, heavy rainfall and the threat of flash flooding will 
move northward over coastal portions of the Carolinas and the 
mid-Atlantic through the weekend. Interests along the U.S. 
East Coast should continue to monitor the progress of this system. 
Additional information on this system, including Gale Warnings, can 
be found in High Seas Forecasts issued by the National Weather 
Service, as well as products issued by your local National Weather 
Service forecast office.
