For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane 
Polo, located a few hundred miles west-southwest of Cabo 
Corrientes, Mexico, on Hurricane Odalys, located well west-southwest 
of the southern tip of Baja California, and on Hurricane Nolo, 
located south of the Big Island of Hawaii.

South of the Gulf of Tehuantepec (EP90):
An area of low pressure south of the Gulf of Tehuantepec continues 
to become better organized as shower and thunderstorm activity 
increases. Environmental conditions are conducive for development, 
and a tropical depression is expected to form in the next day or 
two as the system continues to move slowly to the west-northwest or 
northwest. This motion should keep it parallel to the coast of 
southern and southwestern Mexico.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...90 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...90 percent.

Well East-Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands:
A broad area of low pressure is expected to form within the next day 
or so about 1000 miles to the east-southeast of the Hawaiian 
Islands. Environmental conditions are expected to be conducive for 
development of the system, and a tropical depression is likely to 
form during the early to middle part of next week. The system is 
expected to move generally northeastward, and remain well 
east-southeast of the Hawaiian Islands. 
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...20 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...70 percent.
