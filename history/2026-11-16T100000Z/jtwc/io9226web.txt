MSGID/GENADMIN/JOINT TYPHOON WRNCEN PEARL HARBOR HI//
SUBJ/TROPICAL CYCLONE FORMATION ALERT (INVEST 92B)//
RMKS/
1. FORMATION OF A SIGNIFICANT TROPICAL CYCLONE IS POSSIBLE WITHIN
080 NM EITHER SIDE OF A LINE FROM 11.6N 87.0E TO 13.7N 84.8E
WITHIN THE NEXT 12 TO 24 HOURS. AVAILABLE DATA DOES NOT JUSTIFY
ISSUANCE OF NUMBERED TROPICAL CYCLONE WARNINGS AT THIS TIME.
WINDS IN THE AREA ARE ESTIMATED TO BE 28 TO 33 KNOTS. METSAT
IMAGERY AT 160730Z INDICATES THAT A CIRCULATION CENTER IS LOCATED
NEAR 11.6N 85.8E. THE SYSTEM IS MOVING NORTH-NORTHWESTWARD AT 07
KNOTS.
2. REMARKS: THE AREA OF CONVECTION (INVEST 92B) PREVIOUSLY LOCATED NEAR 
10.7N 86.7E IS NOW LOCATED NEAR 11.6N 85.8E, APPROXIMATELY 338 NM EAST-
SOUTHEAST OF CHENNAI, INDIA. ANIMATED MULTISPECTRAL SATELLITE IMAGERY 
DEPICTS A PARTIALLY-EXPOSED, ELONGATED LOW LEVEL CIRCULATION CENTER 
(LLCC) WITH DEEP, PERSISTENT CONVECTIVE BANDING SHEARED TO THE NORTHWEST 
OF THE LLCC AND A FLARING PUFF OF CONVECTION OVER THE LLCC. A BULLSEYE 
160301Z ASCAT-B PASS SHOWS 25-30 KNOTS ALONG THE SOUTHERN AND WESTERN 
FLANKS OF THE ELONGATED LLCC. WHILE STILL CONSOLIDATING, THE ASCAT 
REVEALS THE RADIUS OF MAXIMUM WINDS IS CONSTRICTING AND THE LLCC IS 
CONSOLIDATING. ENVIRONMENTAL ANALYSIS REVEALS A RELATIVELY FAVORABLE 
ENVIRONMENT FOR DEVELOPMENT WITH MODERATE TO HIGH VERTICAL WIND SHEAR 
(VWS) OF 20-25 KNOTS, STRONG UPPER-LEVEL OUTFLOW, AND WARM SEA SURFACE 
TEMPERATURES (SSTS) OF 29-30C. GLOBAL DETERMINISTIC AND ENSEMBLE MODELS 
ARE IN GOOD AGREEMENT THAT INVEST 92B WILL CONTINUE TO CONSOLIDATE AS IT 
TRACKS NORTH-NORTHWESTWARD OVER THE NEXT 24 HOURS. GFS REMAINS THE MOST 
AGGRESSIVE SOLUTION, FORECASTING 92B TO REACH CRITERIA WITHIN THE FIRST 
24 HOURS WHILE THE CONSENSUS MAINTAINS STEADY DEVELOPMENT.
MAXIMUM SUSTAINED SURFACE WINDS ARE ESTIMATED AT 28 TO 33 KNOTS.
MINIMUM SEA LEVEL PRESSURE IS ESTIMATED TO BE NEAR 1004 MB. THE
POTENTIAL FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF A SIGNIFICANT TROPICAL CYCLONE
WITHIN THE NEXT 24 HOURS IS HIGH.
3. THIS ALERT WILL BE REISSUED, UPGRADED TO WARNING OR CANCELLED BY 
170730Z.//
