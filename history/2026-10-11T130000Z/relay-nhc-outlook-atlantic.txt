Eastern Tropical Atlantic:
An elongated area of low pressure centered a few hundred miles 
southwest of the Cabo Verde Islands is producing a large but 
disorganized area of showers and thunderstorms extending from these

too strong for development by midweek. Regardless of development, 
heavy rainfall will be possible during the next few days over 
portions of the Cabo Verde Islands as the system moves slowly 
northward to north-northwestward.
