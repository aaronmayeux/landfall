For the eastern and central North Pacific east of 180 longitude:

Active Systems:
The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Major 
Hurricane Polo, located less than 200 miles offshore of southern 
Mexico, and on Tropical Storm Odalys, located well west-southwest of 
the southern tip of Baja California.

Southeast of the Hawaiian Islands (Former TD 15-E):
Showers and thunderstorms associated with the remnants of Tropical 
Depression Fifteen-E, located roughly 430 miles south-southeast of 
South Point, Hawaii, are gradually becoming better organized. 
Environmental conditions are conducive for development, and a 
tropical depression is expected to form in a day or so, while the 
system moves slowly west-northwestward. Heavy rains associated with 
this system are forecast to spread over the southeastern Hawaiian 
Islands starting on Wednesday and continuing through Saturday, 
possibly resulting in life-threatening flash flooding and mudslides 
for portions of the Big Island and Maui. Interests in the Hawaiian 
Islands should continue to monitor the progress of this system.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...high...90 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...90 percent.

South of the Gulf of Tehuantepec:
An area of low pressure is expected to form in a couple of days to 
the south of the Gulf of Tehuantepec. Afterwards, environmental 
conditions should be favorable for gradual development, and a 
tropical depression is likely to form late this week or this weekend 
as the system moves slowly west-northwestward to northwestward, 
parallel to the coast of southern and southwestern Mexico.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...high...70 percent.
