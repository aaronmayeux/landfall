1. FORMATION OF A SIGNIFICANT TROPICAL CYCLONE IS POSSIBLE WITHIN
100 NM EITHER SIDE OF A LINE FROM 13.6S 54.5E TO 12.4S 50.8E

ISSUANCE OF NUMBERED TROPICAL CYCLONE WARNINGS AT THIS TIME.
WINDS IN THE AREA ARE ESTIMATED TO BE 25 TO 30 KNOTS. METSAT
IMAGERY AT 070600Z INDICATES THAT A CIRCULATION CENTER IS LOCATED
NEAR 13.6S 54.3E. THE SYSTEM IS MOVING WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD AT 04
KNOTS.
2. REMARKS: THE AREA OF CONVECTION (INVEST 94S) PREVIOUSLY LOCATED NEAR 
13.6S 55.4E IS NOW LOCATED NEAR 13.6S 54.3E, APPROXIMATELY 392 NM 
NORTHEAST OF TOAMASINA, MADAGASCAR. ANIMATED MULTISPECTRAL SATELLITE 
IMAGERY (MSI) AND A 070331Z 91 GHZ MICROWAVE IMAGE DEPICT A 
CONSOLIDATING LOW-LEVEL CIRCULATION CENTER (LLCC), WITH DEVELOPING 
CENTRAL CONVECTION. ELEVATED WINDS ALONG THE SOUTHERN PERIPHERY INDICATE 
A MORE FAVORABLE CHANCE OF DEVELOPMENT AS SEEN IN A 070435Z METOP-B 
ASCAT PASS. ENVIRONMENTAL ANALYSIS DEPICTS A MARGINALLY FAVORABLE 
ENVIRONMENT WITH WARM SEA SURFACE TEMPERATURES OF 29-30C, LOW TO 
MODERATE VERTICAL WIND SHEAR OF 15-20 KTS, AND GOOD POLEWARD OUTFLOW 
ALOFT. GLOBAL ENSEMBLE MODELS ARE IN AGREEANCE WITH 94S TRANSITING 
SLOWLY WESTWARD. GLOBAL DETERMINISTIC MODELS WITH THE EXCEPTION OF ECMWF 
ARE REMAINING CONSERVATIVE WITH MINIMAL DEVELOPMENT BEFORE MAKING 
LANDFALL OVER NORTHERN MADAGASCAR. MAXIMUM SUSTAINED SURFACE WINDS ARE 
ESTIMATED AT 25 TO 30 KNOTS. MINIMUM SEA LEVEL PRESSURE IS ESTIMATED TO 
BE NEAR 1000 MB. THE POTENTIAL FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF A SIGNIFICANT 
TROPICAL CYCLONE WITHIN THE NEXT 24 HOURS IS HIGH.
3. THIS ALERT WILL BE REISSUED, UPGRADED TO WARNING OR CANCELLED BY 
080630Z.//
