MOVING SOUTHWARD AT 08 KNOTS.

178 NM NORTHWEST OF KWAJALEIN. ANIMATED MULTISPECTRAL SATELLITE IMAGERY 
(MSI) DEPICTS A CONSOLIDATING CIRCULATION WITHIN PERSISTENT DEEP 
CONVECTION BEGINNING TO BUILD AROUND THE CENTER. ENVIRONMENTAL ANALYSIS 
REVEALS LOW VERTICAL WIND SHEAR (10-15 KTS), WARM SEA SURFACE 
TEMPERATURES (29-30 C), AND MODERATE POLEWARD OUTFLOW ALOFT. GLOBAL 
DETERMINISTIC AND ENSEMBLE MODELS ARE IN AGREEMENT IN THE GRADUAL 
DEVELOPMENT OF 97W. MODELS DEPICT A GENERALLY QUASI-STATIONARY POSITION 
WITH A SOUTHWARD COMPONENT AS THE SYSTEM SLOWLY CONSOLIDATES OVER THE 
NEXT 24 HOURS. MAXIMUM SUSTAINED SURFACE WINDS ARE ESTIMATED AT 18-23 
KNOTS. MINIMUM SEA LEVEL PRESSURE IS ESTIMATED TO BE NEAR 1005 MB. THE 
POTENTIAL FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF A SIGNIFICANT TROPICAL CYCLONE WITHIN 
THE NEXT 24 HOURS IS HIGH.
