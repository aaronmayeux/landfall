Dot height starts at 1.065 — the exact plane the hurricane globe's node mesh floats on. Glow size is how far out the light reaches, which is also how much room it has to fade. Lower softness = wider fade.

Every pair except Ember stays clear of the fixed hazard colours — no Saffir-Simpson hue, no watch/warning hue, nothing sitting on the USGS shaking scale. Ember is your reference image, for comparison.

At true speed a wave takes about 95 minutes to cross the planet, and out at space distance it moves a twentieth of a pixel a second. That is why the multipliers are here.

Tap the planet to put a wave where you tapped. Drag to spin, pinch or wheel to zoom. Keyboard: arrows spin, +/− zoom, Enter fires, Esc resets, 2 and 3 switch worlds.
